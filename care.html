THE CARE GUIDE

A little care goes a long way.

Understand the essentials of plant care and create an environment where your plants can thrive.

☀ 01 · LIGHT

Let there be light.

Light is one of the most important factors in plant health. Learn the difference between direct, indirect and low light.

Watch how your leaves respond.
💧 02 · WATER

Water with intention.

Overwatering is one of the most common problems for indoor plants. Check the soil before reaching for your watering can.

When in doubt, check the soil.
🌱 03 · SOIL

Healthy roots matter.

Good soil should provide both moisture and air. Choose a mix appropriate for your particular plant.

Drainage is essential.
💨 04 · HUMIDITY

Think about moisture.

Tropical plants often appreciate more humidity, especially in dry indoor environments.

Group tropical plants together.
✂ 05 · PRUNING

Remove what is fading.

Removing damaged or dead leaves can help maintain the appearance of your plant and encourage healthy new growth.

Always use clean tools.
🪴 06 · REPOTTING

Give roots room.

Plants may eventually outgrow their containers. Learn to recognize when roots need more space.

Choose a pot only slightly larger.
FOR BEGINNERS

Start simple. Observe often.

You do not need to know everything about plants from day one. Start with one easy plant, observe its leaves and soil, and slowly learn how it responds to your environment.

COMMON PROBLEMS

When your plant looks unhappy.

01

Yellow Leaves

Often related to watering problems, but check the plant's overall environment before changing your routine.

02

Brown Tips

Dry air, inconsistent watering or mineral buildup can sometimes cause brown leaf edges.

03

Drooping Leaves

Check the soil moisture, light and temperature before assuming the plant simply needs water.

04

Slow Growth

Insufficient light, seasonal changes or limited nutrients may affect growth.
